# Design information and record checks

Checked 22 October 2026. This system checks recorded evidence and operational exceptions. It does not assess drawings, certify designs or decide that legal duties have been met.

| Rule | Check | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| DESIGN-INFORMATION | Safety-critical active revisions without a purpose, risk reference or safe-use reference. Approval is blocked until references exist. | WorkSafe New Zealand's Health and Safety by Design guidance, section 3.3 and Table 1, describes designer information duties under HSWA section 39. The three-field test is our evidence prompt, not the statutory test. |
| FILE-REFERENCE | Active revisions without a file reference. | Internal document-control rule. |
| REVIEW-OVERDUE | Pending review past its assigned due date. | Internal deadline, not a statutory period. |
| SUPERSEDED-HANDOVER | Handover includes a superseded revision. | Internal revision-control rule. |

[WorkSafe: Health and Safety by Design](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/dmsdocument/3951-health-and-safety-by-design-an-introduction), section 3.3. The guidance addresses information on intended use, testing and conditions necessary for safe use. The operator must inspect and supply the actual information. Reference text alone proves none of this.

A reviewer records acceptance or rejection with an evidence reference. All assigned reviews must accept before the latest revision can be approved. Approval supersedes the previous approved revision and leaves old handovers visible. The actor label does not authenticate a qualified engineer.

Imported records default to safety-critical for review. A qualified operator can classify a draft drawing using update-drawing before any approval exists. An Australian or other jurisdiction needs its own applicable rules agreed before operational use. This build does not claim jurisdiction-wide compliance.
